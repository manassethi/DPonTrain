How the simulator works

Each day d before departure, expected bookings are

λ = D₀ × level × curve[d] × (fare ÷ ₹1,100)−ε

and actual bookings are a Poisson draw from λ, capped at the seats left.

  • Realcurve[d]: share of 3A passengers booking d days out, journeys Mar–Jul 2025.
  • Reallevel: how busy each departure was vs an average day, and the calendar forecast available before booking opened.
  • AssumedD₀ and the elasticity ε. Fares never changed in the data, so these are scenarios.

The pricer solves V(d, s) = max over fares of E[fare × bookings + V(d − 1, s − bookings)] backwards from departure, giving the best fare for every combination of days and seats left.
